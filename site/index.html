Each cluster is one doctor on the Thoracic Oncology Service, and each point is one of their most cited papers (OpenAlex). Point size scales with citations. Within a cluster, recent papers sit near the core and older ones trail out along the arms. A paper written by two or more doctors on the service sits in the cluster of the one who led it (first author, otherwise last author), ringed in all their colors and tied by thin lines to the other authors' cores. Color runs from violet for the longest-publishing doctors to gold for the newest. Rays mark papers cited this week, one ray per new citation. Hover for details, click a paper to open it in OpenAlex.
